A little about the numbers.

Clear sources. Honest gaps.

From the source

Australian football and rugby league draws use AFL and NRL data. Basketball, NFL and tennis use ESPN feeds. Racing uses Formula 1, Jolpica and the official Formula 2 and Formula 3 sites. Historical coverage varies by competition and season.

Updates, with context

Data is refreshed automatically and served from saved snapshots. An update time helps you judge freshness. Provider delays can affect scores, session results, penalties and player statistics; the viewer labels unavailable or incomplete data where known.

Built around the season

Schedules and ladders follow the season released by each provider. Future draws, playoff opponents and next-season information appear when published. A confirmed result can change after an official correction.

An independent companion

cgrillo is an independent sports viewer. Team names, competition names and logos belong to their respective owners. For official decisions or the latest competition announcements, follow the relevant organiser’s source.

Your sport, on your device

Star a competition, team or driver to shape your homepage. These preferences stay in this browser on this device using local storage; cgrillo does not send them to a server. You can unstar an item or reset your selection from the homepage. With no favourites, the usual overview appears.
