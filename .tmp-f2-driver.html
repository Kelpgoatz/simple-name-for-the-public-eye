BIOGRAPHY

After winning multiple karting trophies, Rafael Câmara moved up to single seaters in 2022, competing across the UAE, Italian and ADAC Formula 4 Championships. He finished second, third and third in each respectively before moving onto Formula Regional Middle East and European Championship by Alpine in 2023. Those campaigns brought third and fifth overall, and he returned to both the following year and secured third overall and then the FRECA title in impressive fashion. He added the FIA F3 title with TRIDENT to his record with an impressive campaign.

In 2026, the Scuderia Ferrari Driver Academy talent steps up to FIA Formula 2 with Invicta Racing.
